ACTION

Ball to Wall

Throw a ball at the monsters on the wall before time runs out. Every hit scores, and the monsters keep coming.

What you can change

  • Five ready-made worlds: space station, haunted castle, jungle temple, underwater city and candy land, or your own background
  • Up to five monsters, from the ready-made set or your own pictures
  • Game length, number of targets and points per hit
  • Easy, Normal or Hard
  • Score, countdown and sound effects on or off

CALM & PLAY

Projection Pond Pro

A living pond for the floor or wall. Koi, goldfish, trout, perch, carp and catfish swim and turn like the real thing, frogs kick, newts walk and diving beetles row. Hit a fish to score.

What you can change

  • Four ponds: Japanese koi, woodland freshwater, tropical lagoon and moonlight pond
  • Pond shape: wide oval, circular, rounded rectangle, natural curved or freeform
  • Pond size, water movement and ripple strength
  • Show or hide the pond outline

EXPLORE

Ocean Explorer

An underwater world with ten lifelike sea creatures, from clownfish and seahorses to a puffing pufferfish. Touch the water to make ripples and bubbles, and find the hidden creatures.

What you can change

  • Five scenes: tropical coral reef, crystal lagoon, kelp forest, sunken shipwreck and deep ocean
  • Choose which sea creatures swim in your ocean
  • How they swim: natural, gentle and slow, lively, or in shoals
  • Bubbles, background movement, ripple strength and touch sensitivity
  • Sound on or off

CHALLENGE

Ultimate Shooting Range

Three games in one: Target practice, Drone invasion and Duelling tree. Play solo or in teams, with scores and difficulty levels.

What you can change

  • Five ranges: industrial warehouse, neon arcade, forest range, hi-tech training bay and urban rooftop
  • Targets: bullseyes, metal plates, energy orbs, drones or a mix of them all
  • How targets move: side to side, up and down, zigzag or unpredictable
  • Easy, Normal, Hard or Expert, or your own settings
  • Solo or two teams, over one round, best of 3 or best of 5

TEAMS

Paint Splat Wall

Every hit leaves a splat in your team's colour. The team covering the most wall when the timer ends wins. Or switch to free paint and just make a mess.

What you can change

  • Team Battle, or Free Paint with no timer
  • Two or three teams, with rounds from 8 to 30 seconds
  • Four walls: neon grid, charcoal, light brick and artist's canvas
  • Small, medium or large splats, with dripping paint if you like
  • A 3-2-1 countdown, and an attract screen that invites players while nobody is playing
Included in the download ↓
LEARNING

Constellation Connect

Hit the stars in order to draw a constellation that comes alive. Learning games for ages 3 and up.

What you can change

  • Counting, the alphabet, little letters, skip counting (2s, 5s or 10s) and sums up to 10 or 20, or just for fun
  • Stars in order or shuffled
  • Star size: small for accurate throwers, large for young children
  • Hints that name the next star or make players think, always, after a mistake, or never
Included in the download ↓
REVEAL

Touch and Uncover

Brush away a magical mist to reveal the picture underneath. Choose any picture you like, from a photo of your venue to a party theme.

What you can change

  • Any picture you like as the hidden picture
  • Mist colour: ocean blue, mystic purple, pearl white or sea glass
  • Brush size and mist thickness
